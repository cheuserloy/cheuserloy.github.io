# Group leader

## Christoph Heuser-Loy

**Junior Research Group Leader | University Hospital Bonn**

I lead the Immunoengineering Lab at the University Hospital Bonn. Our research focuses on the physiology and engineering of therapeutic T cells, with the aim of understanding and improving their function in cancer immunotherapy.

We combine approaches from cellular immunology, molecular biology and synthetic biology to investigate how T-cell state and function can be shaped and how these insights can be translated into improved cell therapies.

My research interests include:

- **T-cell physiology:** understanding the cellular programmes that determine T-cell fitness, persistence and functional state.
- **Metabolism and exhaustion:** investigating how metabolic constraints and chronic stimulation shape therapeutic T-cell function.
- **T-cell engineering:** developing genetic and synthetic-biology approaches to tune and control T-cell behaviour.
- **Cell therapy:** translating mechanistic insights into strategies to improve adoptive T-cell therapies.
